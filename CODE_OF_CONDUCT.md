# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want **Audit Trail for Filament** to stay a welcoming, respectful place for developers of every background and experience level. Be kind, assume good intent, and criticise code — not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Use inclusive, professional language.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Respect differing viewpoints and the amount of volunteer time maintainers invest.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the topic, or bad-faith contributions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainer through the repository's contact channels. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

This is a short, original code of conduct inspired by the spirit of community standards used across open source.
